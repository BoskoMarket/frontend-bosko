## Objective

Make the client home look like the reference: headline, a standalone search bar (outside any card), category chips, a featured card with a person, and one colored card per trade with a cut-out photo of a professional of that trade.

## Decisions

- Images: generated by Codex (`codex exec`, image generation), 7 transparent PNGs in `assets/images/categories/` (`plomeria`, `electricidad`, `gas`, `construccion`, `fotografia`, `clases-particulares`, `hero`).
- Trades come from `GET /categories` (6 today). Image chosen by normalized category name; unknown categories fall back to the emoji/icon card.
- Card tints are new design-system tokens (light + dark), never literals in the screen.
- Search stays a trigger to `/search` (no input on the home), now as its own pill below the headline.
